# Contract Compliance Policy

**Document ID:** CONTRACT_COMPLIANCE_2025_1  
**Version:** 2025.1  
**Effective:** 2025-01-01  
**Expires:** 2025-12-31

## Section 1 Scope and Contract-Required Categories

This policy governs contract coverage for eligible purchase orders. In this MVP, the contract-required categories are IT Services and Professional Services. Other categories may use contracts, but absence of a contract alone is not a failure under this policy.

<!-- PAGE BREAK -->

## Section 4.1 Required Contract Coverage and Approved Exceptions

For an eligible purchase order dated during 2025 in IT Services or Professional Services, a valid contract at issuance is required unless a documented contract exception was approved by a DIRECTOR no later than issuance. A documented, timely Director-approved exception is a permitted substitute for a valid contract for the specific purchase order.

## Section 4.2 Valid Contract Evidence

A valid contract is resolved to the purchase order, matches its supplier and category, covers the issuance date, and has not terminated on or before that date. An expired, terminated, mismatched, or unresolved contract reference is not valid contract coverage.

<!-- PAGE BREAK -->

## Section 5 Documentation and Escalation

The purchase record should preserve the contract reference or the documented exception and its approval timing. When contract evidence is unavailable or a reference cannot be resolved, Procurement must investigate the record before a conclusion is made. This policy does not treat supplier concentration as a contract violation.
