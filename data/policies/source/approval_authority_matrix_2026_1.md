# Approval Authority Matrix

**Document ID:** APPROVAL_MATRIX_2026_1  
**Version:** 2026.1  
**Effective:** 2026-01-01  
**Expires:** Current

## Section 1 Purpose and Applicability

This matrix defines purchase approval authority for eligible purchase orders issued or closed during its effective period. It does not replace contract, supplier qualification, or competitive-sourcing guidance.

## Section 2 Valid Approval Criteria

A valid PURCHASE approval is recorded as APPROVED on or before the purchase order issuance date. Each required approval must be from a distinct approver. A rejected, withdrawn, pending, late, or duplicate approval does not satisfy this matrix.

## Section 4.2 Purchase Orders Strictly Above USD 50,000

For an eligible purchase order dated on or after 2026-01-01, a total amount strictly greater than USD 50,000 requires two distinct valid PURCHASE approvals, including at least one valid approval by a DIRECTOR. The amount comparison is strict: an amount equal to USD 50,000 is not within this section.

The requirement is assessed against the committed purchase order amount and the approval record available at issuance. This section is the authoritative hard approval requirement for the stated scope.

<!-- PAGE BREAK -->

## Section 5 Recordkeeping and Timing

The purchase record must retain the approver identity, role, approval type, status, and timestamp. When approval evidence is unavailable, the record requires review rather than an assumption that no approval occurred. Questions outside this matrix should be escalated to Procurement Governance.
